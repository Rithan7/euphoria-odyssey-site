About the fest

A grand cultural fest

Euphoria is the annual cultural extravaganza of PSG College of Arts & Science. It is a fest built around passion, wit, performance, and the rewarding experience of coming together on one platform.

Sixteen events span two arenas: off-stage contests that test strategy, knowledge, and craft, and on-stage performances that reward nerve and presence. Every participant who walks in becomes part of the same crew for a day.

Before you set sail

General rules

  • College ID and Participant ID must be worn inside the campus at all times.
  • No on-spot registration is allowed — register through the website and obtain your Registration ID. It's mandatory to enter the venue.
  • Participants must maintain decorum and strictly follow their event's specific rules.
  • Violation of any rule leads to immediate disqualification.
  • Venues are announced at the registration desk — report there by 8 am.
  • Report to your event venue 15 minutes before it begins.
  • Lunch is provided; collect your food token with your participant tag at the desk.
  • Certificates go to every participant; cash prizes go to the winners of each event.
  • The college winning the most points takes home the Overall Trophy.
  • The judges' decision is final and binding.
  • Only one team per college may register for each off-stage and on-stage event.
